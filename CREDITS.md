## VFX, shaders and UI art — original

No third-party art or VFX packs are used. All of the following are
generated at runtime from code in this repo:

- **Particles:** cartoon pickup/skid/exhaust effects (`Players/KartParticles.cs`,
  `KartPickupEffect.cs`, `KartSkidEffect.cs`, `KartSpeedEffect.cs`),
  landmark glow + sparkle (`World/LandmarkGlow.cs`), obstacle ground-ring
  beacons (`World/TrackObstacle.cs`).
- **Shaders:** toon/outline shaders (`Assets/Resources/Shaders/`), Ghibli sky,
  cloud, lit and post-process shaders (`Assets/Shaders/MarioKart/`), paper
  grain and speed lines (`Rendering/PaperGrainEffect.cs`,
  `Rendering/SpeedLinesEffect.cs`).
- **UI art:** halftone paper, inked panel frames, speech bubbles, word bursts,
  starburst countdown and HUD (`UI/ComicStyle.cs`, `UI/GenerationUI.cs`,
  `UI/RaceHUD.cs`), obstacle signs (`World/ObstacleVisuals.cs`).
- **Meshes:** kart body, driver, wheels, placeholders, track and indoor room
  are primitive geometry built in code (`Players/KartVisual.cs`,
  `World/TrackMeshBuilder.cs`, `World/IndoorRoomBuilder.cs`, …).

## 3D models fetched at runtime

None are committed to the repo; they are proxied through the backend per
world and discarded on restart.

- **Meshy** (<https://www.meshy.ai>) — image-to-3D / text-to-3D meshes for
  the objects in the player's photo and description, only when "Generate
  personalized assets" is on. Ownership/usage of generated output is
  governed by Meshy's terms for the account whose `MESHY_API_KEY` is used.
- **Poly Pizza** (<https://poly.pizza>) — generic low-poly filler models
  (CC0 / CC-BY, per model) found by keyword search. The per-model author
  and licence fields from the API are **not yet surfaced** in the game
  (known gap, see [ADR 0009](docs/decisions/0009-generic-filler-from-poly-pizza.md));
  anything distributed with retrieved models should credit their authors
  from Poly Pizza's `Attribution` field.
