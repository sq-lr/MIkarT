Kart sounds, from Freesound:

- kart_engine.wav (3.97s)
    "Noise: Cartoon Car Engine" by NicknameLarry -- https://freesound.org/s/487508/
    steady putt-putt, the one engine loop: KartAudio pitches it up to rev. (A synth 'racing' clip was tried as a second layer and dropped -- it sweeps in pitch, so it audibly restarts every loop.)
- kart_screech_0.wav (0.84s), kart_screech_1.wav (0.93s), kart_screech_2.wav (0.52s), kart_screech_3.wav (0.75s), kart_screech_4.wav (0.78s), kart_screech_5.wav (0.85s)

    a run of separate mouth-made 'skrrt's -- split into one-shot variants
- kart_hit_0.wav (0.58s), kart_hit_1.wav (0.48s), kart_hit_2.wav (0.40s), kart_hit_3.wav (0.43s)
    "TOONImpt - 4 Strong hits with a little tail" by JustWatson64 -- https://freesound.org/s/866950/
    four toon clangs about a second apart
- pickup_powerup.wav (1.60s)
    "Powerup 3" by JustInvoke -- https://freesound.org/s/138491/
    organ jingle; tail past 1.6 s is silence
- pickup_powerdown.wav (1.85s)
    "Power Down" by noirenex -- https://freesound.org/s/159399/
- countdown_beep.wav (0.50s)
    "Short beep countdown" by gurie -- https://freesound.org/s/368779/

- countdown_go.wav (2.30s)
    "airhorn-short.wav" by guitarguy1985 -- https://freesound.org/s/68999/
    quick air horn with its echo, for GO!
